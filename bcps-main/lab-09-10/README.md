# Labs 09–10: Educational presentation

**Aim:** Create a presentation in LibreOffice Impress using a theme, slide transitions, animations and multimedia.

## Files

- [computer-fundamentals.odp](computer-fundamentals.odp): seven editable slides for presenting.
- [computer-fundamentals.pdf](computer-fundamentals.pdf): static preview, including the quiz answers.
- [three-notes.wav](three-notes.wav): original 1.8-second audio clip, also embedded in the ODP.

## Open and present

1. Open the ODP in LibreOffice Impress.
2. Press **F5** to start. Use Space or a click to advance and Esc to finish.
3. On slide 2, click three times to reveal input, process and output examples.
4. Slide 6 has a three-note sound attached to its transition. Keep the system volume on.
5. On slide 7, click to reveal the answers.

The PDF cannot play sound, transitions or animation. Use the ODP for those features.

## Practical procedure

1. Create a widescreen presentation with seven slides.
2. Use a navy master background, cream text and teal headings throughout.
3. Add a title and clear text to each slide. The input–process–output diagram consists of editable text objects.
4. Open **Slide Transition** in the sidebar and choose the horizontal wipe transition. Apply it to every slide.
5. Open **Animation**, select each example block on slide 2 and add an **Appear** entrance effect with **On click** timing. Repeat for the answer on slide 7.
6. Select slide 6. In **Slide Transition**, choose a sound using **Other sound** and the supplied WAV. This delivered ODP already contains the sound inside the document package.
7. Save as ODP. Export a PDF for a static preview.

## Result and checks

The finished deck covers the input–process–output cycle, hardware and software, memory and storage, operating systems, multimedia and a short quiz.

The ODP reopened with seven slides, all seven transitions and four native animation effects. Its embedded WAV remained present after a LibreOffice save, and LibreOffice recognised it as an allowed package sound. Every PDF slide was visually reviewed for contrast, clipping and overlap. Author and modified-by fields are blank.

Audible playback and slideshow timing have not been observed on the desktop. If your platform cannot play the embedded clip, the supplied WAV can be played separately. All text and the diagram remain editable.
